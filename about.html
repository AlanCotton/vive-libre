About Vive Libre

We've been exactly where you are now.

The excitement, the spreadsheets at midnight, the quiet worry that you'll get something important wrong. We did it ourselves, and built Vive Libre so you don't have to do it alone.

Our story

We learned the hard way, so your way is easy.

A few years ago, we were the clients. We fell for the coast on a sun-soaked holiday and decided to make it home. What followed was a crash course in everything nobody warns you about: the agent who works for the seller, the render that flattered a north-facing plot, the paperwork that stalls for weeks because one document wasn't quite right.

We got there in the end. And we arrived with a little black book of the people who actually deliver, and a clear view of where the journey goes wrong.

Vive Libre is that experience, handed to you. A partner who's been through it, on your side from the first conversation to your first morning here.

Meet the team

The people you'll actually be talking to.

Founder · Property search & relocation

Estelle

Ten years as a property search agent in London, working with overseas investors, time-short professionals and buyers who wanted a stress-free move.

Her relationships with the top exclusive agents in central London meant off-market opportunities came to her first, and her honed negotiation secured the best property at the best price.

She has brought every bit of that to the Spanish market, ready to help with your move.

Off the clock: creating art and writing poetry, practising yoga, and long walks in the beautiful countryside.

Founder · Business & negotiation

Jane

A successful entrepreneur of 25+ years, Jane has founded businesses across AI, training & development, sales, marketing & GTM, and property & interiors.

As a female founder who created an entire business sector (outsourced go-to-market for agencies), she was asked by the last UK government to write a paper on the future of small business.

She brings exceptional business and negotiation skills to help you create your new life on the Costa Blanca.

Off the clock: travel in and beyond Spain, two Tibetan Terrier puppies that keep her busy, and writing a book.
