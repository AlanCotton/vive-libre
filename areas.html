Areas we cover

The Costa Blanca North, town by town.

From Villajoyosa up to Dénia, each town along this coast has its own character, light and pace of life. Here's how to think about where you might belong, and we'll help you feel the difference in person.

Alteathe jewel of the Costa Blanca

Altea, the jewel of the Costa Blanca.

Whitewashed, romantic and quietly creative, Altea is the most beguiling town on the coast. Cobbled old town stacked above a blue-domed church, a working harbour, and pebbled bays where the waves draw back with a hush. Altea draws artists and people who want beauty over bustle: characterful, walkable and genuinely lived-in.

Morairaquiet sophistication

Low-rise, low-key, gently exclusive.

A planning ethos that kept the high-rises out, so Moraira stays intimate: a pretty marina, good restaurants, sheltered coves. A long-standing favourite for those who want calm with everything close to hand.

Jávea (Xàbia)three towns in one

Historic centre, port and arenal beach.

Sheltered beneath the green Montgó massif, Jávea offers a beautiful old town, a fishing port and a sandy bay. Three distinct moods in one place. Established, leafy and especially loved by international families.

Also along this coast

Seven more towns worth knowing.

Villajoyosa

Candy-coloured fishermen's houses and a chocolate-making heritage at the coast's southern edge.

Finestrat

A dramatic mountain village inland, with sea-view villas and a foot in both worlds.

La Nucía

An easygoing inland town of whitewashed plazas and palms, with space, sport and value, minutes from the sea.

Albir

A calm pebble bay below the Serra Gelada, with its lighthouse walk and a palm-lined promenade.

Calpe

Defined by the Peñón de Ifach rock, with long beaches, salt flats and flamingos.

Benissa

A handsome historic town inland with its own rugged, unspoilt stretch of coast.

Dénia

A lively port city with a castle, a gastronomy scene and the ferry to Ibiza.
